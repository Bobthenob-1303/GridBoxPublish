Mechanical Textile Machinery

During the Industrial Revolution, machines such as the Spinning Jenny, water frame, spinning mule, and power loom mechanized textile production. Instead of one person manually spinning thread or operating a hand loom, machines could perform these processes much faster and on a much larger scale..

Redundancy

Before mechanization, textile production could be done in homes or small workshops. A skilled weaver manually controlled the loom and produced fabric relatively slowly..
